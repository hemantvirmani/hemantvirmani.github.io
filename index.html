Jul 2021 - Jan 2026
At Amazon's Bellevue, WA office, I led Engineering and Science for Customs and Compliance across Amazon Global Logistics. The question underneath all of it: how do you make sure every package crossing a border plays by the rules, whichever country's rules those happen to be? Over 4.5 years: expanded from US-only to UK, EU, and Japan, across three Amazon businesses; built a science team from scratch in 2024; shipped an agentic AI system that cut classification costs by 95%. That last one still surprises me. It's what happens when you approach an old manual process like you've never seen it before.
Jul 2014 - Jul 2021
At Amazon's Hyderabad, India office, my work evolved over seven years in a way I didn't fully anticipate. It started with infrastructure: a real-time transportation monitoring platform handling 10K+ TPS. Then shifted toward something I found more interesting: computer vision and document extraction for seller verification, including forgery detection, work that later became AWS Textract's AnalyzeID API. In between, I re-architected Amazon's de facto secure document storage system, the kind of foundational work nobody sees but everything depends on. The question I kept coming back to: how do you know the person signing up to sell is actually who they say they are? By the time I was leading in-person seller identity verification end-to-end, all of it had compounded into a system that onboarded 2M+ businesses and cut fraud to a sixth of what it was.

Led a research team to launch Adobe LeanPrint, co-authored six US patents, shipped a PS/PDF-to-XPS converter in print pipelines, and delivered the long-awaited Multiple Undo feature in Adobe FrameMaker, among other platform and product improvements.
